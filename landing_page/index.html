The "Greenwashing Detector" Tool

In cosmetics, ingredients are listed by concentration. If a brand promotes "Argan Miracle Oil", but Argan is ingredient #18 on the label, the actual concentration is below 0.1%! Test marketing claims here:

Greenwashing Severity Score High Discrepancy
Estimated Active: < 2.5%

The first 3 ingredients are synthetic polymers and silicones (Dimethicone). The natural active ingredient is merely marketing decoration.
